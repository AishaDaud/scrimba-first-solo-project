POWERPUFF G❣RLS

The Powerpuff Girls[c] is an American animated superhero television series created by Craig McCracken for Cartoon Network. The show centers on Blossom, Bubbles, and Buttercup, three kindergarten-aged girls with superpowers. They live in the fictional city of Townsville with their father and creator, a scientist named Professor Utonium.

Bubbles

Bubbles (voiced by Tara Strong in the series) is one of the main characters in the animated television series "Powerpuff Girls".

Buttercup

Professor Utonium

Professor Utonium is the deuteragonist of The Powerpuff Girls. He is the scientist responsible for creating his daughters, The Powerpuff Girls. He also teaches classes in quantum physics and micro-nuclear fusion at Townsville Research Center.
